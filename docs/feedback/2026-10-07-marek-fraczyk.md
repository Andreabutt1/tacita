# Feedback: Marek Fraczyk (colleague, 120 Ventures) – 2026-10-07, 9:12 AM

Context: Google Chat message the morning after the pitch (2026-10-06). Marek proposes segmenting by *value of the knowledge* and *how often it has to be transferred*, then validating with one contact per segment.

## Three segments

1. **Knowledge not that valuable, but transferred repeatedly.**
   Example: restaurants, high staff churn, new staff need to learn basics of service quickly.
   What matters: **speed of knowledge transfer** and **low price**.

2. **Knowledge rather valuable, transferred repeatedly.**
   Example: real estate maintenance, water management services. Workers deal with a wide range of tasks including specialised devices.
   What matters: **speed, price, and accuracy**.

3. **Knowledge of extreme importance, proprietary, must be transferred exactly as is.**
   Example: highly specialised manufacturing (electronics manufacturing), anything involving highly specialised tools like advanced CNC machining devices. Providers of such tools have training programmes, but Tacita would fill the gap of what the departing staff learned on top.
   What matters: **confidentiality and accuracy**. Speed is not that important.

## Proposed next step

Get a contact person for each segment and ask:
- Is this a pressing issue for them?
- How do they deal with it today?
- What would their ideal scenario be?

"Overall, I think it's worth checking it out with real business owners as I believe you're onto something. And the best part is, the worst thing that can happen is that you learn something else about how businesses operate."
